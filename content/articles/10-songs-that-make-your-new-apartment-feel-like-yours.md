Sometimes it happens when you hang something crooked on the wall.

Sometimes it's the first meal you cook badly in your own kitchen.

Sometimes it's 11:47 PM, you're sitting cross-legged on the floor because the furniture still isn't assembled, and you're listening to a song that suddenly makes the empty room feel like it belongs to you.

So forget the packing playlist for a second.
# These are songs for the becoming-at-home part.

## 01. For the first morning you wake up there

# ☀ Here Comes the Sun by The Beatles

There is something almost offensively optimistic about waking up in a new apartment and seeing sunlight land on a floor that, yesterday, belonged to nobody.

You make coffee.
You open the curtains.
You stand there barefoot for no particular reason.

And suddenly the whole thing feels possible.
> "Little darling, it's been a long, cold lonely winter."
This is the song for that first little exhale.

The boxes can wait.

# The apartment is yours now!

## 02. For unpacking boxes at midnight

# ☀ Sweet Disposition by The Temper Trap

There is a very specific kind of 1 AM energy that appears when you've been moving all day and suddenly decide:
You know what? Let's finish the kitchen.

You are tired.
Your hair is doing something hostile.
There is a half-open box labelled MISC that contains absolutely no discernible logic.

*And somehow you're happy.*

Sweet Disposition has that suspended, slightly breathless quality that belongs to nights when you're not entirely sure what comes next, but you're excited about it anyway.
> "Won't stop 'til it's over."
Put it on.

Keep unpacking.
Pretend tomorrow doesn't exist!

## 03. For cooking your first real meal

# ☀ Put Your Records On by Corinne Bailey Rae

The first meal you cook in a new kitchen has absolutely no obligation to be impressive.
It can be pasta.
It can be eggs.

It can be something you found on the internet at 6:30 PM because you suddenly realised you've eaten crackers for lunch.

Put music on.

Open a window.

Let something simmer.

This song makes an ordinary evening feel a little more golden around the edges.
> "Put your records on, tell me your favorite song."
*There.*

Now your kitchen has a soundtrack.

## 04. For dancing around an empty living room

# ☀ Dog Days Are Over by Florence + The Machine

There is nothing quite like having an empty living room and absolutely no reason to behave.

No sofa yet?
Excellent.

*More dancing space!*

No coffee table?
*Perfect.*

No neighbours who know you yet?
*Even better.*

This is the song for opening the windows, turning the volume up and dancing around a room that still has your moving boxes stacked against the wall.
> "Happiness hit her like a train on a track."
Maybe your apartment doesn't look finished.

Who cares?
# You're already living in it!

## 05. For when you suddenly miss your old home

# ☀Ribs by Lorde

Nobody warns you that getting what you wanted can come with a strange little ache.

You can be thrilled about your new place and still miss the old one.
You can want independence and still want to crawl into your childhood bed for an evening.
You can be excited about the future while grieving the version of life you just left behind.

That's allowed.

Ribs understands that particular feeling of time moving faster than you're emotionally prepared for.
> "It feels so scary getting old."
Put this one on when the new apartment feels a little too quiet.

Then call someone you love.

## 06. For the "holy shit, I actually live here" moment

# ☀ Home by Edward Sharpe & The Magnetic Zeros

Eventually, something clicks.

Maybe you're brushing your teeth.
Maybe you're watering a plant.
Maybe you're standing in the kitchen waiting for the kettle.
Nothing dramatic happens.

You simply look around and realise:
*I live here.*

Not I'm staying here.
Not I'm moving here.

# *I live here.*
> "Home is wherever I'm with you."

And suddenly the word home feels less like a building and more like a feeling you've been quietly constructing.

## 07. For cleaning when you've decided to become a responsible adult

# ☀ Golden by Harry Styles

There is a particular kind of delusion involved in deciding that today is the day you become A Person Who Keeps Their Apartment Clean.

You put on music.
You open the windows.
You find cleaning supplies you forgot you bought.

Suddenly you're wiping down countertops like someone is filming a lifestyle documentary about you.
Let yourself enjoy it.
> "You're so golden."
The laundry can wait five minutes.

*You're having a montage.*

## 08. For sitting on the floor because the furniture isn't assembled yet

# ☀ Bloom by The Paper Kites

This one is softer.

The boxes are still there.
The curtains aren't up.
The furniture instructions have become a personal insult.

So you sit on the floor.

Maybe you order food.
Maybe you stare at the ceiling.
Maybe you realise you actually like the room better than you expected.

Bloom belongs to those quiet moments when nothing particularly exciting is happening, but something is beginning.
> "Can I be close to you?"
Sometimes becoming comfortable in a place happens quietly.

No fanfare.
Just you, the floor, and the first signs of a life taking shape.

## 09. For the first rainy evening

# ☀ Dreams by Fleetwood Mac

Rain does something strange to a new apartment.
*It softens everything.*

The unfamiliar walls.
The traffic outside.
The silence.

You make something warm.

Turn the lights down.

Put the kettle on.

And suddenly you're looking through your own window while rain slides down the glass, thinking about everything that brought you here.
> "Thunder only happens when it's raining."
This is the one for staring dramatically out the window.

Dramatic staring is free.
# Use it generously.

## 10. For the moment it finally feels like home

# ☀ Vienna by Billy Joel

Not because you've finished.
You probably haven't.

There may still be a box sitting in the corner that you have decided is now part of the furniture.
But eventually you stop trying so hard to arrive.

You start simply living.

You make breakfast.

You leave a mug in the sink.

You know which floorboard squeaks.

You have a favourite corner.

You know what the light looks like at 5 PM.

You have routines.

Little rituals.

*A life.*

And maybe that's the point of Vienna here.
> "Slow down, you crazy child."
You don't have to get everything right immediately.

You have time.

## Your apartment doesn't become home all at once.
It happens in fragments.

The first song.

The first coffee.

The first dinner.

The first terrible DIY attempt.

The first rainy evening.

The first time you dance around the living room.

The first time you miss the place you came from.

The first time you look around and realise you've stopped calling it the apartment.

*It's just home now.*

So put something on. Open the windows. Sit on the floor if you have to.

Let the boxes wait.

You're allowed to take your time becoming at home. 

P.S. If your new apartment currently contains three boxes, one mattress, a dying plant and absolutely no curtains, congratulations.
You're doing beautifully.

Home, Finally is for everything that happens between "I got the keys" and "I actually live here."
