## Alcohol Regulation Buddy

A structured space for understanding your relationship with alcohol.

Alcohol Regulation Buddy is a **CBT-based, self-guided digital system created by a mental health professional**. It is designed to help you look at drinking as a pattern you can observe, understand and work with, rather than simply something you are supposed to stop doing through willpower.

Inside, you'll find tools for understanding the alcohol loop, identifying triggers, examining thoughts, tracking urges and drinking episodes, practising regulation strategies, and building a more informed picture of what is happening underneath the behaviour.

### Standard

**The complete core system.**

Standard gives you the full Alcohol Regulation Buddy system, including:

- My Starting Point
- My Alcohol Loop
- Trigger tracking
- Thought tracking
- Urge tracking
- Drinking Log
- The Alcohol Promise
- Regulation Toolbox
- Interactive exercises and tools
- Safety First
- Support & Treatment information

It is the complete working system for people who want a structured place to understand and work with their drinking patterns.

### Premium

**The complete system + deeper visual insight.**

Premium includes everything in Standard, plus an analytical layer designed to help you see your patterns more clearly over time.

Additional features include:

- Deeper pattern capture
- Advanced visualizations
- Pattern charts
- Richer tracking views
- Additional analytical views
- Requires a paid Notion plan. Premium uses advanced Notion features for its visualisations, charts and deeper analytical views.

Premium is for people who want to go beyond recording what happened and start looking at what their collected patterns may be showing them.

### Built from mental-health knowledge and lived reality

Alcohol Regulation Buddy was created by a mental health professional and is informed by CBT principles, clinical mental-health knowledge, and real-world lived experience of alcohol problems.

The system has been developed through hands-on use and refinement.

It is a self-guided educational and behavioural tool, not a substitute for professional assessment or treatment.

### A place to look closer

You don't need to have everything figured out before you start.

Sometimes the useful question isn't simply:

**"How do I stop drinking?"**

It is:

**"What is actually happening here?"**

Alcohol Regulation Buddy gives you somewhere to investigate that question.
