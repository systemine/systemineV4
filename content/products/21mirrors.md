# Each chapter is a different mirror. 
Some are difficult to look into. Some might feel unexpectedly familiar. And some may show you something you didn't realize you'd been carrying.

Written at the intersection of psychology, storytelling, and mysticism, the book doesn't approach mental health as a list of symptoms to diagnose. Instead, it asks a more human question: what does it actually feel like to live inside these experiences?

At the heart of it all is Angelique, the enigmatic woman who sits across from each of these souls and listens. Through her, the stories become less about what's "wrong" with someone and more about what happened to them, what they carry, and what healing might look like.

There are 21 stories, covering experiences ranging from body dysmorphia, panic disorder and depression to attachment wounds, autism, BPD, trauma, addiction, NPD, bipolar disorder, ADHD, limerence and grief.

This isn't a clinical handbook.
It isn't a self-help workbook.
And it isn't a book asking you to have everything figured out.

It's a collection of lives, wounds, strange corners of the mind, and little moments of recognition.

*Some mirrors are sharp. Others heal. All of them are human.*
